# Upgrade Calcit to 0.13.77

## English

- Migrate the legacy compact/full dual Snapshot layout to the canonical `calcit.cirru` entry configuration while retaining `compact.cirru` until both entries pass their behavior gates.
- Align Calcit and `@calcit/procs` at 0.13.77, replace moving module refs with released versions, and move CI to Node 24 plus Yarn 4.
